# Brainstorm Navigator

Brainstorm Navigator is a lightweight prototype that guides leadership teams from an end objective through structured Q&A, market dynamics, public status signals, and facilitated brainstorming.

## Features
- Capture the end objective for the session.
- Gather business model understanding through guided Q&A prompts.
- Simulate auto-researched market dynamics with curated insight cards.
- Track public status layers such as funding, hiring, and sentiment.
- Translate visible challenges into structured brainstorming prompts.
- Present a slide outline and facilitation chat to guide leaders through the session.

## Quick start
Open `index.html` in your browser. No build step is required.

## Next steps
Hook the market dynamics and status layers into live public data sources (news APIs, funding databases, job boards) to automate insights.
