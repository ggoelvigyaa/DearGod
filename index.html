Brainstorm Navigator

Guide teams from objective to structured brainstorming.

Capture the end goal, uncover the business model through Q&As, summarize market dynamics, and translate challenges into action-ready brainstorming prompts.

Session overview

  • Objective
  • Business model Q&A
  • Market dynamics (auto sourced)
  • Business status layers
  • Challenges & expansion
  • Slides + chat facilitation

1. End Objective

Define the outcome leadership wants to achieve.

2. Business Model Q&A

Answer guided prompts to help the system understand the business model.

3. Market Dynamics (auto sourced)

Pull in public signals about the market landscape.

4. Business Status Layers

Capture public-facing indicators such as funding, customer sentiment, and hiring signals.

5. Visible Challenges

List challenges and expand on them with context from leadership.

6. Brainstorming Facilitation

Generate slides and guide the discussion with a built-in chat.
